# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

Written 2026-11-17 from what Pete said while redoing his resume and LinkedIn profile. He was not
interviewed for this file; lines marked (inferred) are my reading and his storyboard answers
correct them.

## Platform

web

## Users

- Primary: a hiring manager, founder or recruiter at a mission-driven organization (health,
  schools, towns, climate, public benefit) who has Pete's application, resume or LinkedIn open
  and gives this site about a minute to decide whether to talk to him. Mostly on a laptop. From
  the LinkedIn app, on a phone. (inferred)
- Secondary: people who meet Pete or one of his products and want to see what else he built.
  (inferred)

## Product Purpose

The personal site of Pete McCarthy at www.peteknowsai.xyz. It shows that he ships working AI
products himself, says what kind of role he wants, and makes it easy to reach him or read his
resume. Success is a visitor who opens a live product, opens the resume, or contacts him.

## Positioning

A product leader with twenty years of experience and a founder exit who now builds and ships AI
agents himself: four live products and three client projects since January 2025 as a one-person
shop, with coding agents doing most of the typing.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive from his LinkedIn profile, whose About ends by pointing here, from his resume,
  and from the website field of job applications.
- The site, the LinkedIn profile and the resume tell one story. They must not disagree on a fact.
- He lives in Paonia, Colorado and works remote. He wants a full-time role.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One static site. No backend, no forms, no analytics.
- Hosted on Cloudflare Pages and deployed by hand with wrangler. A push to main deploys nothing.
- Only the www address resolves today. The bare domain has no web record.
- Undecided until Pete answers the storyboard: whether paid consult bookings stay, which portrait
  the site uses, and whether the public resume shows his phone number.

## Brand Commitments

- The name "Pete Knows AI", the handle @peteknowsai and the domain peteknowsai.xyz.
- His LinkedIn banner, approved by Pete on 2026-11-17: a black field, a row of flat ticks that
  turn into rising bars at the word "AI", and the four product names on the tallest bars.

## Evidence on Hand

- Live products: Cells (cells.md), HomeZero (homezero.md), Transparent Towns (ttowns.org),
  Yeoman (yeoman.md).
- Client work: CommonLight, Port32, Peggy.
- Career facts and numbers come from his resume, which lives outside this repo.
- Portraits: `public/images/profile-v2.jpg`, a painted-style picture, and his LinkedIn photo.
- There are no testimonials, press mentions, customer logos or usage numbers for the 2025
  products. Never invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Show the working thing. Every claim links to something live.
2. One minute. A visitor learns who he is, what he built and how to reach him without hunting.
3. One story. The site, LinkedIn and the resume agree on every fact.
4. A job, not gigs. Nothing on the page sells hourly work. (inferred, pending Pete's answer)

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable by keyboard and screen reader, every link labeled, and no motion for a visitor who has
asked their device to reduce it.
