# Site redo Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the link list at www.peteknowsai.xyz with the one-page letter Pete locked in storyboard round 5.

**Architecture:** One static `index.html` with one stylesheet, built by Vite into `dist/`. No framework, no JavaScript on the page, no request to any other host. The app-builder stack (React, Tailwind, TypeScript, the Mocha plugin) is deleted. Cloudflare Pages keeps serving it, uploaded by `wrangler` from main.

**Tech Stack:** Vite 6, plain HTML and CSS, two self-hosted variable fonts from `@fontsource-variable/*`, `node --test` for checks, `wrangler pages deploy` for upload.

**Spec:** `specs/designs/2026-11-17-site-redo.html` (round 5, locked 2026-11-17 with M3). Its frames `L1`, `L2`, `P1`, `P2` and `M3` are the page; its Calls C1 to C20 are requirements. Frame styles are in `specs/designs/site-redo.css` under `.fam`, the round 4 block and the round 5 block. The direction contract is in `.impeccable/surfaces/index-html.md`.

## Global Constraints

- The page names no town: the strings "Paonia" and "Colorado" appear nowhere in `index.html`, its meta tags or `og.png` (C19).
- The only booking link is `https://cal.com/peteknowai/30min` (the account is spelled `peteknowai`; it answers 200). No paid consult, no 15-minute slot, no word "consult" (C7).
- Nothing loads from another host: no script, stylesheet, font or image URL that is not root-relative. No analytics, no form (C12).
- Nothing moves except on hover or focus, and nothing moves at all under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` (C13). The four characters hold still (C20).
- Copy is the storyboard's, word for word. Do not reword a sentence.
- Colors: page `oklch(0.975 0.008 95)`, ink `oklch(0.2 0.01 95)`, rule `oklch(0.2 0.01 95 / .14)`, highlight `oklch(0.92 0.07 150)`. Faces: Roboto Slab (300 for the letter, 400 for product names), Archivo (400, 600, 700).
- Canonical address is `https://www.peteknowsai.xyz/`. Every absolute URL in the head uses it (C10).
- The repo is public. No phone number and nothing private in any file, commit or PR text.
- No dark theme and no extra section: every frame ships and nothing else does.

## Review Focus

1. A width between the two drawn frames (641 to 1100px): the headline or the history row overflows the page. Expected: no horizontal scroll at any width from 320 to 1920. Test in Task 2, QA case 7.
2. A slow connection: the slab face arrives late and the headline jumps. Expected: layout shift under 0.1. Preload in Task 2, measured in QA case 13.
3. A long word or a 200 percent zoom on a phone: the history row's company and years collide. Expected: they wrap, never overlap. QA case 6.
4. A link preview fetched by a crawler that ignores `www`: `og:image` must be absolute and on `www`. Test in Task 3.
5. A keyboard visitor: every link reachable in reading order with a visible ring. QA case 9.
