# Home page (index.html)

Scope: the one page of www.peteknowsai.xyz. Mode: Persuade.

Audience: a hiring manager, founder or recruiter at a mission-driven organization, arriving from
Pete's LinkedIn, resume or an application, giving it about a minute. Laptop first, phone second.
Job: decide whether to talk to him. Action: open a live product, open the resume, email him or
book the free 30-minute call. Proof: four live products, three clients, twenty years of history
with numbers. Constraints: one static page, no backend, no forms, no tracking, no third-party
requests, nothing moves except what the visitor points at, the page names no town, and the only
booking is the free 30-minute call.

Memorable moment: the headline with his painted portrait set inside it, then four small
characters in a row that turn out to be four live products.

Unresolved: none for the page. His resume and LinkedIn differ on three facts, which he has been
asked about; the page follows LinkedIn.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is one signed letter from Pete, set in the look his four products share. It
refuses the portfolio grid of case-study cards and the link-in-bio list it replaces.

OWN-WORLD: A pale warm page, near-black ink, and one soft green highlight behind two phrases.
Roboto Slab Light for everything the letter says; Archivo for the bar, the history list and
small captions. Hairline rules at 14 percent ink. One dark filled button, for the resume. No
boxes, no cards, no section headings. The only pictures are his painted portrait, round, inside
the headline, and each product's own character.

STORY: The visitor learns that Pete builds and ships AI agents himself, sees four live products,
scans twenty years of history, reads what he wants next, and emails, books a call or opens the
resume.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A bar with the name left and Email, Book a call, Resume right. "Hi, I'm Pete."
Then the headline "I build AI agents [portrait] and ship them myself." at 88px on two lines, one
paragraph with the first highlight, and four characters in a row, each a link to a live product.
The primary action is the resume button, top right.

FORM: A signed letter. Pinned by Pete across storyboard rounds 3 to 5; none of the rolled worlds
was taken. seed: dcafe70eee054f67

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
